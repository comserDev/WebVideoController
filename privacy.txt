Privacy Policy for AAGAG Video Controller

1. Overview
AAGAG Video Controller ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices regarding the Chrome Extension.

2. Information Collection and Use
- No Personal Data Collection: The Extension does NOT collect, store, log, or transmit any personal information, user identifiers, browsing history, or user data.
- Local Operation Only: The Extension operates entirely within your local browser. It modifies the web page DOM on aagag.com solely to inject video playback speed controls and a play/pause button.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties
Since no data is collected, we do not share, sell, or transfer any user data to third parties.

4. Permissions
The Extension requests host permissions for https://aagag.com/* strictly to run local content scripts that render the video control interface on video elements.

5. Contact
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the Chrome Web Store developer contact email.
